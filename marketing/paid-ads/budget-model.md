## Break-Even Analysis

**Assumptions:**
- Average order value: $70
- Cost of goods (product + shipping): ~$20
- Gross margin per order: ~$50

**Break-even CPA: $50**

Any CPA below $50 is profitable on first purchase. With repeat purchases
(peptide research is recurring), the true break-even CPA is higher.

**With estimated 30% repeat rate and 2.5 orders/year:**
- Customer LTV: ~$175
- LTV gross margin: ~$125
- Acceptable CPA for new customer: up to $80 (1.5:1 LTV:CAC)
- Target CPA for healthy growth: $25–$35 (3.5:1–5:1 LTV:CAC)

---

## Scaling Rules

1. **Never increase budget more than 30% at a time** — platform algorithms
   need time to adjust
2. **Wait 3–5 days between budget changes** — let learning phase complete
3. **Scale by duplicating winning ad sets**, not just increasing budget
4. **Monitor frequency** — if Meta frequency > 3, audience is saturated
5. **Watch for CPA creep** — if CPA rises 20%+ after a budget increase,
   pull back

## Cash Flow Consideration

Platform billing is typically net-30 or threshold-based. With crypto payments
from customers, there may be a cash flow gap. Plan for:

- Google Ads bills monthly or at threshold ($500 default)
- Meta bills at threshold or monthly
- Crypto payments from customers may take 1–3 confirmations
- Budget the ad spend as a fixed monthly cost, separate from revenue
