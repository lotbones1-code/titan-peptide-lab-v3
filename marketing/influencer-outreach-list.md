## Compliance Safeguards — MANDATORY for every partnership

Put this in the sample kit as a physical card + email it before the product ships. Refusal to comply = no second sample, we claw back the affiliate.

### What influencers MUST say (non-negotiable)

- **"For research use only."** — Stated verbally and/or on-screen in any video, and in captions of any post.
- **"Not medical advice."** — Any mention of use must include this or equivalent.
- **"I'm not a doctor / this is not a recommendation for anyone else."** — If they describe their own experience.
- **"Results are my own, not typical, not guaranteed."** — Attached to any outcome claim.
- **Disclose the relationship.** — #ad or #gifted or "Titan sent this free" (FTC requirement — on them legally, but we enforce).

### What influencers CAN'T say (will get us both in trouble)

- **No human dosing recommendations.** Not "take 2 sprays twice a day." Not "this is the dose you want." Not "start at X mcg." Zero dosing guidance for humans.
- **No therapeutic or disease claims.** Not "cures inflammation," "treats depression," "reverses aging," "heals injury," "fixes gut," "replaces TRT," "treats ED / anxiety / PTSD." Nothing that sounds like a drug indication.
- **No structure/function claims beyond what the label says.** Label says "research chemical." That's the ceiling.
- **No comparisons to FDA-approved drugs as superior.** Never "better than Ozempic / Viagra / Xanax."
- **No claims about injectable equivalence.** Nasal ≠ injectable bioavailability — don't promise same results.
- **No "legal" framing.** BPC-157 is on the FDA Category 2 bulk list. Don't say it's "FDA approved" or "legal for human use."
- **No pediatric, pregnancy, or chronic-illness-population content.** Zero. If the creator has that audience, pass on the partnership.
- **No stacking protocols marketed to their audience.** They can say what they personally stack, framed as research log — they can't publish a "stack guide."

### Our internal checklist before greenlighting an influencer

- [ ] Audience is 18+ and predominantly US/CA/UK/AU (check audience insights)
- [ ] No prior history of promoting outright scam supplements (quick Google)
- [ ] Not a licensed medical professional actively practicing (too much regulatory exposure on their end — decline politely)
- [ ] Agreed in writing (DM / email) to the compliance card before shipment
- [ ] Tracked in CRM with ship date, post deadline, post URL when live
- [ ] Post reviewed within 48h of going live — if non-compliant language, request edit immediately
